Everyday use

Schedules and the heartbeat.

Agents can do things while you're away: check a build every morning, watch a page, chase a deadline. You set it up by asking. And agents with a heartbeat look over the day on their own, and only speak up when something deserves it.

Set one up by asking

Say what to check, how often, and when it's worth telling you. The agent creates the automation and shows you what it set up.

  • Every morning at 7:30, check my repositories and tell me only what changed.
  • Watch this page and tell me when the tickets go on sale.
  • If the backup hasn't run for two days, tell me.
  • Every Sunday evening, ask me how the week went and update the running plan.

To change or stop one, ask the same way: “Move the morning check to 8”, “Stop watching the ticket page”.

Agents pick the simple kind when they can. Schedules run on your computer, while RedLeaf is running. Each schedule also has a rule for the runs it missed while RedLeaf was off.

Seeing what ran

  • Compute › Jobs. Every firing is a job, with when it ran, what it called, how long it took and what it cost. Filter by type or by the app that started it.
  • The agent's Journal. The Scheduled filter shows the runs, and Failed the ones that went wrong.
  • The agent's page. Its Automations field lists every schedule that runs as that agent.
  • The Live discussion. When a check finds something, the agent tells you there.

The heartbeat

An agent with Live turned on has two standing discussions: Live, where it talks to you, and Heartbeat, where it thinks on its own. About once an hour through the day, a heartbeat tick looks at what happened: your discussions, finished jobs, events from other apps. Then it decides whether anything needs doing. Most ticks are quiet, and that's fine.

What a tick may do is set by the agent's Heartbeat Skills. Nova's include:

  • Action items: catch commitments made in conversation and write them down before they're lost.
  • Conversation nudges: prompt the Live discussion to reach out when you've been quiet for a while or something happened worth talking about.
  • Initiatives: start useful work nobody asked for, each in its own discussion titled “Initiative: …”, at most one per tick. Without your permission, the heartbeat does nothing outside your machine, nothing in your name, nothing that spends money or destroys information.

Remove a heartbeat skill from the agent's page and the next tick goes without it. Watch the ticks in the Heartbeat tab, or filter the Journal by Ticks. Turning Live off closes both discussions and pauses the heartbeat, keeping its schedule for later.

Nobody is there to click Allow. Heartbeats and schedules run unattended, so they can't wait for an approval. That's why the default access level is Auto, where a safety check approves routine actions and blocks risky ones. See Access levels.
