Everyday use

Discussions and step squares.

A discussion is one conversation with one agent. It keeps its history, its session and what was shared in it. The step squares are its record: every action the agent took, with what went in and what came out.

Starting and finding discussions

Choose New in the Discussions sidebar or press Ctrl+N. Pick the agent, then the provider and quality for this discussion, and choose Go. With several agents, a filter at the top of the sidebar shows one agent's discussions at a time.

  • Unread discussions have a teal dot and a bold title.
  • The spinner on each row shows its state: gold while the agent is thinking, pink while work it handed off is running, amber when it is waiting for your answer or approval.
  • Search every discussion with Ctrl+Shift+F. A result opens the discussion at that message.
  • Close a discussion with Alt+W to archive it. Find it again under More › Browse archives, and choose Reopen to continue.

Sending, queueing and interrupting

You want toDo this
SendEnter. Shift+Enter for a new line.
Stop the agentEsc, or the stop button that replaces send while it works.
Add something for laterType while it works and press Enter. The message waits as a faded bubble and sends when the turn ends. Click it to edit, send it now or cancel it.
Steer the current turnWith providers that support it, Enter joins the turn in progress and Tab queues instead.
Interrupt and sendCtrl+Enter, or right-click the send button.

Drafts are kept per discussion, so you can switch away and come back. A discussion whose session was stopped shows Resume session; sending a message resumes it too.

Attachments and “what I see”

Use Attach image or Attach file, paste an image, or drop files on the discussion. The eye button, Attach what I see, lets you choose exactly what the agent can see with this message: the current RedLeaf page, or one of your monitors. Nothing is captured until you send. Monitors are only offered in the local app, not over remote access.

Anywhere in RedLeaf, right-click something and choose Ask Nova about this to start from it. Artifacts (Alt+A) lists every image, file and link shared in a discussion.

The step squares

Each turn shows a row of small squares, one per step. A square wobbles while it runs, and gets an amber ring while it waits for your approval. Hover for the tool's name; click to open its details.

ColourMeaning
PurpleThinking
TealRead-only: reading files, searching, looking things up
GoldChanging something: editing files, and shell commands
PinkWork handed to another session or helper
RedAn error, or background work that failed or stopped
GreyEvents. Notes and updates are drawn as circles rather than squares.

The details dialog shows the tool, its category, and its Input and Output in full. Large outputs load on demand and can be downloaded. Use ← and → to step through every square in the discussion. Open jumps to the file it touched; Compute opens the job behind it, with who asked for it and what it cost. For work handed to a helper, the dialog shows the helper's own squares too.

This is the audit trail. The squares are not a summary written by the agent. They are the actual calls it made, so you can check what it read before it answered, and what it changed.

Questions and approvals

When an agent asks you something, a question card appears: pick an option, or choose Other and write your own. When it needs permission, an approval card offers Allow once, Allow for this session and Deny. What it may do without asking is set by its access level.

Rewind

With providers that support it, open the menu on one of your messages and choose Rewind to before this message. That message and everything after it leave the conversation, for you and for the agent, and your text comes back to the composer. Files are not changed: anything the agent already did on disk stays.

The discussion menu

Open it with the ⋮ button, or by right-clicking a discussion.

ItemWhat it does
Voice Alt+VOpens voice mode. See Voice.
Info Alt+IProvider, model, quality, access level, cost, tokens and context use. Change the model, quality or access level here.
Rename, Update titleSet a title yourself, or have one written from the conversation.
Share Alt+SCreates a link to a read-only copy.
FloatOpens the discussion in Float Nova, a small window that stays on top.
StopStops the session to free memory. The next message resumes it.
Close Alt+WStops the session and archives the discussion.

In Info, the Confidential switch keeps a discussion out of the Live timeline, the heartbeat, other discussions' context and bulk exports, and hides sharing. The discussion itself stays where it is.
