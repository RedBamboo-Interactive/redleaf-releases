Get started

Your first conversation.

Nova is the agent that comes with RedLeaf. She is the one you talk to: tell her what you're after, and she plans it with you, uses the right app, and remembers what you did together. Here's how to get going.

Open a discussion

  1. Open NovaAfter setup, RedLeaf opens Nova on its own. Later, pick Nova in the app switcher at the top left.
  2. Start a new discussionChoose New in the Discussions sidebar, or press Ctrl+N. Pick Nova, keep the suggested provider and quality, and choose Go.
  3. Say what you wantType in the message field and press Enter. Shift+Enter starts a new line.

Things to try first

  • Here's what I'm working on this month. Help me turn it into a plan, and keep it somewhere we can both find it.
  • Read this contract and tell me what I should worry about. (Attach the file with Attach file.)
  • Remember that I prefer short answers, and that I work on Windows.
  • What can you do in RedLeaf, and what would you need from me to do more?

Give her real things. Nova works best on something you actually care about, and what she learns about it stays in her memory for next time.

Watching her work

While Nova works, each step she takes appears as a small coloured square: reading a file, searching, editing, running a command, handing work to another session. A square wobbles while it runs. Click any square to see exactly what it did, with its input and output, and use the arrow keys to step through the rest. Discussions and step squares explains the colours.

Sometimes she'll stop and ask. A question card lets you pick an answer or write your own. An approval card asks before an action that needs your permission: Allow once, Allow for this session or Deny. How often she asks depends on her access level.

Interrupting and adding to a turn

You don't have to wait. Press Esc to stop her. Type while she works and press Enter to queue a follow-up for when she's done; with providers that support it, Enter adds your message to the current turn and Tab queues it instead.

The Live discussion

At the top of the sidebar, Nova has a standing discussion called Live. It's where she brings things up on her own: a schedule that fired, something that changed while you were away. Next to it you'll find her Heartbeat, the regular moment where she looks at the day and decides whether anything deserves your attention, and her Journal, a day-by-day record of what she did. See Schedules and the heartbeat.

What leaves your computer. Discussions are stored on your PC. When Nova answers, your message goes to the AI provider she uses, along with whatever she reads to answer it. Nothing else is sent.
