Everyday use

Voice and Float Nova.

There are three ways to use your voice with an agent: hold a key and talk, have it speak back, or open a live call. Each needs its own provider, so set up only the ones you want.

FeatureWhat it doesNeeds
Push-to-talkYou speak, it becomes your messageA Speech to Text provider: faster-whisper, local
Spoken repliesThe agent reads something aloud or sends audioA Text to Speech provider: ElevenLabs in the cloud, or Qwen3-TTS locally
Voice modeA live, two-way call with the agentOpenAI GPT-Live, on an OpenAI API account

The local providers need an NVIDIA graphics card and WSL 2, and RedLeaf sets them up for you. See AI providers and local models.

Push-to-talk

When the message field is empty, a microphone button appears next to it: press and hold Hold to talk, speak, and let go. RedLeaf transcribes what you said, tidies filler words and punctuation without changing what you meant, and sends it. If the agent asked you a question, it becomes your answer.

You can also hold a key instead. Set it first: open Settings › Nova and choose a Push-to-talk key under Voice. The default, F13, isn't on most keyboards. The same section has your Microphone, Speaker and Voice processing, which turns on echo cancellation and noise suppression. Turn it off for virtual cables or line inputs.

Spoken replies

With a Text to Speech provider installed, an agent can turn text into speech: ask it to read something out loud, or to record a message. Audio plays right in the discussion. To give an agent a voice of its own, choose one in its Voice field on the agent's page. See Nova and your own agents.

Voice mode

Voice mode is a live call. Open it from the discussion menu with Voice, or press Alt+V. You'll see the agent's portrait, live captions and a status: Listening, Speaking, or Working on that when it's doing something that takes longer. Press Esc or End voice session to hang up.

It isn't a separate conversation. Everything said is saved into the same discussion. Small talk is answered by the live voice model directly. Anything that needs the agent's memory, tools or files is handed to the agent itself, which is when you see Working on that.

Setting it up

  1. Install OpenAI GPT-LiveFrom Settings › Extensions. It's billed per use on an OpenAI API account. A ChatGPT subscription doesn't cover it.
  2. Save a project keyPaste an OpenAI project key, starting with sk-proj-, in the extension's settings.
  3. Choose a Live VoiceOpen the agent's page and pick a Live Voice. Then start Voice from one of its discussions.

If Voice won't start, check that the key is saved and that the agent you're talking to has a Live Voice. Voice mode works on phones too, over remote access.

Float Nova

Float Nova is a compact window with your discussions that stays on top of everything else. Open it with Ctrl+Alt+N, or choose Float in a discussion's menu. While it's open, your push-to-talk key works from any app. Choose Dock to bring the discussion back into RedLeaf.

KeyIn Float Nova
Alt+NNew discussion
Alt+← / →Discussions list / the chat
Alt+↓ / ↑Next / previous discussion

Float Nova is for desktops. It needs the main RedLeaf page to stay open in the background. If it closes by itself, keep RedLeaf open in its own window and press Ctrl+Alt+N again.
