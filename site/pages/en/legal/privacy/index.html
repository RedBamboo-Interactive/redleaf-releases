Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short. RedLeaf runs on your computer and keeps your conversations, files and agent memory there. RedBamboo Interactive does not receive them. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, and the cookbook videos load from YouTube only when you press play. Data leaves your machine only to reach services you choose to use: the AI providers you connect, GitHub for downloads and updates, and our statistics server if you turn on anonymous usage statistics.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual request details (browser, page, time) in order to deliver it, and may keep them in its server logs for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

To show the current release, the download section reads the signed release feed from raw.githubusercontent.com, which GitHub also operates. Nothing else is loaded from third parties until you ask for it: no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no embedded fonts. The demos on this site are animations that run in your browser and send nothing. If you pick a language, your browser keeps that choice in this site's local storage, so the site stops suggesting another language; it is never sent to us or anyone else.

The cookbook videos are hosted on YouTube, which Google operates. A video shows only a still image served by this site until you press play, and nothing is requested from YouTube before then. When you press play, the video loads from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode: from then on Google receives your IP address and the usual request details, and may store information in your browser to play the video. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to Discord, GitHub or YouTube take you to those services, whose own policies apply once you are there.

The RedLeaf software

What stays on your machine

Your RedLeaf account, conversations, agent workspaces and memory, Code sessions, generated images and audio, settings and backups are stored locally, in RedLeaf's database and folders on your computer. RedBamboo Interactive has no access to them.

What leaves your machine, and where it goes

Code sessions run tools such as Git on your machine. When an agent pushes to a remote repository or calls an outside service on your instruction, that data goes to the service you pointed it at.

AI providers

When you use a hosted model, RedLeaf sends your request directly from your computer to that provider. RedBamboo Interactive is not a party to that exchange and does not see it. Each provider decides how long it keeps requests and whether it uses them to improve its models. Read their terms before you connect them, and prefer local providers for material that must not leave your machine.

Legal basis

Delivering this website rests on our legitimate interest in publishing and securing it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Anonymous usage statistics rest on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. The same principles apply under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, object to its processing, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Data RedLeaf stores on your own computer is under your control: you can export or delete it yourself at any time. To exercise a right, write to the address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in France the CNIL, which oversees us; elsewhere in the EU the authority of your country; in Switzerland the FDPIC.
