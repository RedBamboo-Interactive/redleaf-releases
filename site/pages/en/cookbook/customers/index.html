  • Show me every account in the expansion stage.

How it works

  1. Make it your ownIn Database, choose New type and add the fields you need: the stage as a Select, the seats as a Number, the contact and the next step.
  2. See it the way you workIn a Workspace project, add a list of your accounts, or a board grouped by stage.
  3. Briefed before, updated afterAsk for a brief and Nova reads the account and its history. Tell her how the call went and she updates the fields.
  4. Follow-ups that don't slipAsk for a reminder and Nova creates a schedule. When it fires, she posts in Live with the context.
