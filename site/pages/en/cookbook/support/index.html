A simplified recreation of RedLeaf. The community and its questions are examples.

What you can ask

  • How was the community this week?
  • Answer questions in #help. Anything about refunds or contracts comes to me.
  • Three people hit the same import bug. Open a post in #bug-reports with the steps to reproduce.
  • Every Friday at 17:00, write the weekly digest and post the highlights in Live.

How it works

  1. Add a Discord botInstall the Discord extension, create a bot in the Discord developer portal and give RedLeaf its token. The bot speaks as one of your agents.
  2. Choose the channelsEach channel you bind becomes its own conversation, separate from your private discussions.
  3. She answers, you decideNova replies to what she can and brings you the rest: refunds, partnerships, anything you said is yours to decide.
  4. A digest every weekA schedule turns the week into a page in Workspace: the topics, how many threads, and where each one stands.
{{icon:shield-check}}

People on Discord are not you. They can't give Nova orders on your behalf, and she doesn't share your personal information with them. Anything consequential, like a refund, she confirms with you in private first.

What you need

The Discord extensionInstalled from Settings › Extensions.
A Discord botCreated in the Discord developer portal and invited to your server.
RedLeaf runningNova answers while RedLeaf is running on your computer.
