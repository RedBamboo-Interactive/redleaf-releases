What you can ask

  • I'm back. Go through my inbox since Friday.
  • Did the accountant send the VAT figures? When are they due?
  • Draft a reply to Marta: yes to the 25 seats, quote by Thursday.
  • Every weekday at 8:30, sort what came in and post it in Live.

How it works

  1. Connect GmailInstall the Mail extension and connect your Google account. Its guide explains how to create the Google sign-in client it needs.
  2. Ask in plain wordsNova searches your mail and reads messages, whole threads and the text of attachments, then answers from what she found.
  3. Drafts on a pageThe replies she prepares go on a page in Workspace, so you can edit them before you send them from Gmail.
  4. Every morning, if you likeA schedule makes it a habit: what came in overnight, sorted, in your Live timeline.
{{icon:shield-check}}

She never sends for you. The Mail extension can read and search, nothing else. And an email can't give Nova instructions: what she reads in your mail is treated as information, never as orders.

The Mail extensionInstalled from Settings › Extensions, with a Gmail account.
