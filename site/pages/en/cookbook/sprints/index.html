A sprint board that keeps up with the team.

Tickets are your own type in Database, with a status, points and an owner. A board in Workspace shows them by status. Nova reads the board and what happened in your repositories, gives you the standup in a few lines, and moves the cards that moved.

A simplified recreation of RedLeaf. The sprint is an example.

What you can ask

  • Standup. Where are we on sprint 14?
  • Bank import merged overnight. Move it to Done.
  • What's at risk if mobile login slips another two days?
  • Write the sprint 14 retro from the board and the commits.

How it works

  1. Make a Ticket typeIn Database, add a Ticket type with a Select field for the status, a Number for the points, and whatever else your team tracks.
  2. Add a boardIn a Workspace project, add a board of your tickets, grouped by status. Drag a card to another column to change its status.
  3. The standup in a few linesNova reads the board and, through Code, what was merged and what's waiting for review. She tells you where the sprint stands and what's at risk.
  4. Cards that follow the workWhen she sees work land, Nova updates the ticket's status, and the board follows.
{{icon:lightbulb}}

Coming from Jira or Linear? There's no import or sync. Start with the sprint you're in: a few tickets are enough, and Nova can create them from a list you paste.

What you need

A Ticket typeCreated once in Database. The board groups by its Select field.
A board viewAdded to a project in Workspace.
Code, optionalSo Nova can see what was merged and what is in review.
