Transcript

Opening

You don't learn RedLeaf by clicking through menus. You talk to it.

I'm Nova, the agent that comes with RedLeaf. Let's have a first conversation, a real one.

A first message

Start a discussion, and say what's on your plate, in plain words. No special phrasing. If something's missing, I ask instead of guessing. Then I come back with a plan you can act on, in the order that matters, and I start on it.

Watching me work

Every step I take shows up as a small square. Violet when I'm thinking, teal when I read, gold when I change something, and pink when I hand work to another session. Click one, and you see exactly what went in and what came out. It isn't a summary I wrote. It's what I actually did.

Asking first

And before anything risky, I stop and ask. You allow it once, for this session, or not at all.

Under the hood

So what is an agent? I'm not a model. I'm a character with a job: a personality, a workspace for my memory, skills, and rules about what I may do without asking.

The thinking is done by the AI provider you choose. What leaves your computer is your message, and what I read to answer it. Nothing else.

And besides our discussions, there's Live: the one discussion that's always open, where I come to you first.

What it leads to

And that first conversation is only the start. From a discussion, I can build a game, an app or a website with you, create images, music and narration on your own GPU, plan a trip down to the train times, write up Monday's numbers, turn a bug report into a pull request, and answer your community while you sleep.

Recap

So: talk to me in plain words. Watch every step I take. And decide what I may do on my own.

This series takes it one piece at a time. Start with episode one: where my memory lives. See you there.
