Transcript

Opening

Your product launches on Thursday. The store page needs a cover, the trailer needs a voice, and the end card needs a jingle.

I'll make all three with you, from one discussion, on your own graphics card.

Ask for it

Say what the launch needs, in plain words. I write the prompts, pick the settings, and start a job for each piece. You don't need a new tool, and you don't have to leave the discussion.

The covers

For a closer look, the covers are also in Image Studio, an app for image work. Each piece of work is a workstream, and every run in it is kept, with its prompts and seeds. It all renders on your graphics card, so it's free, and the images never leave your machine. You pick the one that works, and the others stay.

Changes, and the sound

Then ask for changes the way you'd ask a person. I keep the prompt and settings behind every result, so I know what to change. The voice-over and the jingle are here too, ready to play.

One wall

Gallery, an app you can add, puts every image and video from across RedLeaf on one wall. Pick, rate and tag them there. It never deletes anything.

Under the hood

So what's underneath? It starts with your request. Each kind of work is a capability: image generation, text to speech, music. On its own, a capability does nothing. A provider fills it in, and you choose which one.

Images run on your graphics card, through a local provider. Voices and music can run there too, or come from a cloud service, on your own account.

On your graphics card, the provider runs a workflow: a saved set of steps, with a version. Each generation is a job you can open, with how long it took and what it cost. The result is an asset, a file kept on your machine and linked to the discussion. It keeps its recipe: the prompt, the seed, the workflow. So anything you liked can be made again.

Recap

So: ask for images, voices and music in plain words. They render on your own graphics card, or a cloud service you choose. And every result keeps its recipe, so you can make it again.
