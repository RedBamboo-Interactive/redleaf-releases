Transcript

Opening

I don't think on my own. A model does, and you choose which one.

So today: a strong model for everyday work, a private one for files that must never leave, and what each job costs.

Connecting a provider

RedLeaf doesn't come with a model of its own. During setup, you choose who does my thinking, and sign in with what you already have: a subscription, or an API key. Once you're signed in, RedLeaf sends one harmless request, to check that I can use it.

One provider per agent

Each agent keeps its own provider. I run on a strong cloud model, for everyday work. And you don't pick model names. You pick a quality tier, from fast to research, and each provider maps it to its own models. Archie, the team's agent for payroll and contracts, runs on a local model instead. For a single discussion, you can still pick another one, right here.

Payroll, at home

So when someone asks Archie to check March payroll, he reads the salary sheet and the contracts, and a model on this computer's own graphics card does the thinking. Nothing leaves the computer.

What it cost

Every session is also a job in Compute, the part of RedLeaf that runs all the AI work. My quote for Brightwell went to the cloud, and you see exactly what it cost. Archie's payroll check ran on the graphics card, for free.

Under the hood

So what happens when an agent thinks? The agent asks, with its provider and its quality tier. Compute starts a session through that provider, and records it as a job.

With a cloud provider, the model runs on the provider's servers. What leaves your computer is the message, and what the agent read to answer it. You sign in with a subscription or an API key, and the sign-in stays in the provider's own tool, on your computer.

With a local model, the session runs on your own graphics card. Nothing leaves, and there's nothing to pay. Either way, each job keeps its provider, its model, and what it cost.

Recap

So: each agent thinks through the provider you pick. Confidential work stays on a local model. And every job shows what it cost.
