Before you start

Every use case begins the same way: install RedLeaf, create your local owner account, and connect one AI provider. Setup walks you through all three. After that, open Nova and say what you want in plain words; she knows which app to use.

Stuck, or found a use case we should add? Tell us on the Leaf Discord.
