Going further

Remote access.

Out of the box, RedLeaf is reachable only from the computer it runs on. If you want your agents on your phone, you can publish it through a Cloudflare Tunnel, with no open ports and automatic HTTPS, and require Google sign-in in front of it.

The options

Open Settings › Network & Access. The Remote Access tab has:

OptionWhat it means
Local onlyThe default. RedLeaf is reachable only from this machine, with no exposure and no other accounts.
Remote via Cloudflare TunnelRedLeaf gets a public address on your own domain, through Cloudflare. Needs a free Cloudflare account and a domain.

The Authentication tab adds Google OAuth sign-in, for remote access or for more than one person.

Set up a tunnel

  1. Create the tunnel in CloudflareIn your Cloudflare dashboard, create a tunnel for your domain and copy its token.
  2. Turn on Google sign-in firstIn Authentication, create an OAuth client in Google Cloud, then paste its Client ID and Client Secret. Switching sign-in restarts RedLeaf.
  3. Connect the tunnelBack in Remote Access, choose the tunnel option, paste the Tunnel Token and the Public Hostname, and save. Tunnel changes apply immediately.
  4. Open it on your phoneVisit your hostname and sign in. Nova can be added to your home screen like an app.
{{icon:warning}}

A public address is a door. Anyone who can reach your hostname reaches your agents' front page. Require Google sign-in before you publish, and keep agents that read untrusted content on a modest access level.

What works away from your desk

  • Discussions, Code, Workspace and the other apps work in a phone browser, with a layout made for small screens.
  • Voice mode works on phones. See Voice.
  • Attach what I see can share the current page, but not your computer's monitors.
  • Float Nova is for desktops only.

Your computer still does all the work, so it has to be on, with RedLeaf running.

More people

Google sign-in is also how more than one person reaches the same RedLeaf, and RedLeaf has three roles for them: Admin, Editor and Viewer. See Database and views. Managing people is still basic in the preview: there's no invitation or user list screen yet.
