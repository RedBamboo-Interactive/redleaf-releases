Everyday use

Images, voices and music.

Ask an agent for a picture, a narration or a song, and it writes the prompt, picks the settings and starts the job. There's no new screen to learn. Each ability comes from a provider extension you install once.

Install them from Settings › Extensions, in the Marketplace. Each one's Guide tab lists what it needs and walks you through setup. Local providers are prepared for you: RedLeaf downloads the program and its models, then tests it with a small job. ComfyUI can also adopt an installation you already have. Ask for it

  • Make three cover ideas for the episode: a lighthouse at night, warm light, painted style.
  • Read the intro aloud in a calm voice and send it to me.
  • Write a 30-second theme for the show, lo-fi, with a little piano.
  • Same image, but wider, for a phone wallpaper.

Results appear in the discussion and are kept on your machine as assets, linked to the conversation that made them. Ask for changes the way you'd ask a person: the agent keeps the prompt and settings it used, so it knows what to adjust.

Find every job

Every generation is a job in Compute. Open Compute › Jobs to see each one with its provider, who asked for it, how long it took, what it cost and where its output went. Filter by type or by app, and re-run or cancel from there.

You can also start a job by hand: in Compute › Capabilities, choose Queue a new job on a capability's card, fill in the settings and choose Generate.

{{icon:cpu}}

One graphics card, several models. Local image and voice models share your GPU and can get in each other's way. The Hardware Monitor at the bottom of Compute shows graphics memory by capability. To free the card, put a capability to sleep with the moon button on its card.

Voices for your agents

Give an agent a voice of its own in its Voice field. Text to Speech keeps reusable voices you can tie to a provider, so the same voice follows the agent across discussions. For live calls, see Voice and Float Nova.

Optional extras

  • Gallery gathers the images and videos from across RedLeaf into one wall, where you can pick, reject, rate and tag them. It never deletes anything.
  • Ambience changes RedLeaf's background, and on Windows your desktop wallpaper, on a schedule, from photos, your own images or generated ones.

Both are in the Marketplace. The Create recipe shows a whole episode made this way.
