A simplified recreation of RedLeaf. The repositories and issues are examples.

What you can ask

  • Every morning at 7:30, check my repositories and tell me only what changed.
  • Watch this page and tell me when the tickets go on sale.
  • Every Friday afternoon, write me a summary of what we finished this week.
  • If the backup hasn't run for two days, tell me.

How it works

  1. Ask in a discussionSay what to check, how often, and when it is worth telling you. Nova creates the automation and shows you what she set up.
  2. Two kinds of checksA simple check polls a web address and compares the answer, at no AI cost. An agent check runs a prompt through your provider when judgment is needed.
  3. The heartbeatNova also has a heartbeat: a regular moment where she looks at what is going on and decides whether something deserves a message.
  4. Results land in LiveYour Live timeline collects what happened while you were away, with the context of where it came from.
{{icon:eye}}

Every run is visible. Each automation keeps its history, and every AI job it starts appears in Compute, so you can see what ran and change or stop it at any time.
