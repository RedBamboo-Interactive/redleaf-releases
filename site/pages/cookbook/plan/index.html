A simplified recreation of RedLeaf. The trip is an example.

What you can ask

  • Plan three slow days in Lisbon in March with one day trip.
  • I launch on the 20th. Work backwards and tell me what has to happen each week.
  • Here's my week. Where do I fit two hours of focused work every day?
  • Remind me a week before the lease notice deadline.

How it works

  1. Start a discussion for itOne topic per discussion keeps the context clean. You can have the trip, the launch and the house move running side by side.
  2. Think it through togetherNova asks what she needs, uses your provider's web search when it has one, and proposes something concrete rather than a list of options.
  3. The plan becomes a pageIt lives in Nova's workspace, so it is still there next month and you can edit it directly.
  4. Reminders that she ownsAsk for a reminder and Nova creates a schedule. When it fires, she posts in your Live timeline with the context, not just a ping.
{{icon:star}}

Ask her to disagree. Nova is set up to tell you when a plan won't work. If the timing is too tight or a step is missing, she says so.
