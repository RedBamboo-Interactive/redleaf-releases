What you can ask

  • Four cover options for episode 12, same style as the last ones.
  • Draw this character from the front, the side and the back so I can keep her consistent.
  • Read the intro in a calm voice, then a faster one, and let me compare.
  • Turn the voice memo I just recorded into notes and a first draft.

How it works

  1. Choose your providers onceCompute lists the capabilities RedLeaf ships with: image generation, text to speech, speech to text and music. Pick a local provider such as ComfyUI, Qwen3-TTS or faster-whisper, or a hosted one such as ElevenLabs.
  2. Ask in the conversationNova writes the prompt, picks the settings and starts the job. You don't have to leave the discussion or learn a node editor.
  3. Watch it in ComputeEvery job shows up in Compute with its provider, progress and output, so you always know what ran where.
  4. Keep what you makeResults are stored as assets on your machine and linked to the conversation. Ask for variations, and Nova knows which one you picked.
{{icon:lightning}}

Local means private and free per image. Local providers run on your own graphics card, so prompts and results never leave the machine and there is no per-image charge. Hosted providers are there when you want a specific voice or model.

What you need

For local imagesA capable graphics card and a ComfyUI installation, connected through the ComfyUI provider.
For local voiceQwen3-TTS and faster-whisper are included as providers.
Or hostedAn ElevenLabs account for voices, or another supported provider.
