A simplified recreation of RedLeaf. Leni is an example agent.

What you can ask

  • Practise German with me at B1, and correct one mistake at a time.
  • Be my running coach: ask how the week went every Sunday and adjust the plan.
  • Play a sceptical investor and attack my pitch.
  • Quiz me on the chapters I read this week.

How it works

  1. Create an agentGive it a name, a personality and a purpose in its identity, and choose the skills it may use. It gets its own workspace and memory.
  2. Start a discussion with itPick the agent when you open a new discussion. Each one keeps its own history, separate from Nova's.
  3. Use your voiceSpeak instead of typing; speech to text can transcribe it locally. The agent can answer aloud with the voice you chose.
  4. It tracks your progressThe agent writes what it notices into its workspace, so the next session starts where the last one ended.
{{icon:users-three}}

Agents can work together. Nova can see what your other agents are doing and pass work between them, so your tutor and your coding sessions never need you as a messenger.

What you need

An AI providerAny supported chat provider. Each agent can use a different one.
For voiceThe included local speech providers, or ElevenLabs.
