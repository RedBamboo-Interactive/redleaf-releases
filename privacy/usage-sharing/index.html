Leaf usage sharing: privacy notice

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who is responsible

Laurent Bécherel (RedBamboo Interactive), Zurich, Switzerland, is responsible for usage sharing. Contact: becherel.laurent@gmail.com.

What is collected, and only if you opt in

Usage sharing is off by default. If you turn it on, your installation sends one small report per day for each question listed in Settings > Usage sharing. A report contains a random identifier, the question and its version, the day, and counts grouped by fixed categories (for example “Codex: 3 sessions”). If you choose to share with a name, the name you typed is stored with your identifier. Reports never contain your messages, prompts, generated content, file or project names, titles, account details or exact times. Your IP address is not stored.

Why

To understand how testers use Leaf so it can be improved. Each question says why it is asked.

Legal basis

Your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); Swiss FADP). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Usage sharing, and withdrawing is as easy as giving it.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare’s infrastructure, in a database restricted to the European Union. Cloudflare processes it on Laurent’s behalf. Nobody else receives it.

How long

While a question is active and you are sharing. When a question is retired, the answers linked to people are deleted after 30 days; only totals across at least five testers may be kept, with no identifier. Identifiers with no report for 180 days are deleted.

Your rights

You can see everything you send in Settings > Usage sharing, export it with your account data, delete it at any time with Delete my data, and contact Laurent with any question. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Switzerland, the FDPIC; in the EU, your national authority).
